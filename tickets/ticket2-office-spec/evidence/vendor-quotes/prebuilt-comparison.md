## What is gained by buying pre-built

- One ready-to-use system from one vendor, with the vendor listing a one-year warranty on the WW-KZ builds.
- Less time spent selecting, assembling, troubleshooting and validating individual parts.
- A single system-level support contact may simplify warranty claims.
- HP OmniDesk includes Windows 11 Home SL and wired keyboard/mouse in the listed package.

## What is lost by not building

- Less control over exact motherboard, PSU, cooling, case, storage and memory models.
- Less ability to optimise each PC to its employee's workload.
- The Developer pre-built includes an RTX 3050 that is not needed for the assumed development workload, while its listing identifies an H610 board and DDR5; verify exact motherboard model and upgrade options.
- The Designer pre-built has only 8 GB GPU memory, half the 16 GB in the custom proposal. This matters for larger 3D scenes and GPU-accelerated workloads.
- HP OmniDesk is limited to 32 GB RAM according to the listing, while the custom workstation platforms have more upgrade flexibility.
- The total pre-built price exceeds the 3,000,000 KZT ceiling by 619,500 KZT, whereas the custom plan remains 111,030 KZT under budget before peripherals, assembly and delivery.

## Important listing caveats

1. The WW-KZ i5-14400 page lists the processor as 6 cores, whereas Intel's i5-14400 specification is 10 cores (6 performance + 4 efficiency). Treat this as a catalogue data error and request written confirmation from Shop.kz before using the quotation.
2. The WW-KZ i5-14400 and Ryzen 7500F pages explicitly list the operating system as absent. The comparison adds the same 82,990 ₸ Windows 11 Home USB licence used in the custom budget.
3. No monitor is included in these desktop-system listings. The comparison adds the monitors from the custom build.
4. The HP OmniDesk includes keyboard and mouse; no equivalent peripheral cost has been added to the custom build. This makes the pre-built comparison favourable to the pre-built option.
5. The Shop.kz pages show online-store prices; request formal quotations and confirm availability, delivery, warranty and exact component models before purchase.
